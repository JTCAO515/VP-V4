# ADR-0010: Two-city content pilot

Status: accepted on 2026-08-24 through explicit operator delegation.

## Decision

The first content pilot is Beijing as curated city and Guangzhou as ordinary city. Each import/review batch is capped at ten externally sourced candidates. No draft, candidate, or city coverage claim becomes public without source rights, identity resolution, Fact review, and projection evidence.

## Consequences

- Current Guangzhou research drafts remain ineligible research workbench material.
- AI-23/AI-24 own import and publication; AI-29 is the release gate.
- Batch growth requires a new capacity and provenance review.

## Rollback

Stop the pilot, keep candidates private, and invalidate any public projection derived from a failed review gate.

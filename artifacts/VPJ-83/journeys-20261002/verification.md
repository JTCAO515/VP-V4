## Implemented

- Journeys reads current-conversation goals and saved Trips in distinct sections. Goal text is literal owner-read content, with its version; Trip titles/versions come from the existing list. No date, task-status or recommended decision is inferred. Open current VP conversation and Open Trips reuse existing consumers.
- A goal relation is unlinked only after an explicit valid link reply with null Trip fields. Missing/malformed/stale/foreign/unknown replies stay unknown. A current linked Trip must match the returned conversation/goal/version and an owned list ID/head. No goal or Trip writer is added.
- Read publication is atomic, scope/generation-fenced and bound to a 20s request-start deadline. Leaving the tab/scene clears it. A policy reread before publication rejects a mid-read withdrawal. Trip reading remains independently available when goal/consent reading fails. Missing inputs never become empty-success claims.
- Clicking a saved Trip captures its scope and ID. The approved original Trip entry reuses reload/select, requires the ID in that same-owner list, rejects cancellation or scope replacement before selection, and displays unavailable for absent/failed selection. It never silently selects another Trip. Default nil preserves the old entry, and a simultaneous initial planning request is rejected as ambiguous. Existing explicit Trip actions remain in their original consumer.

Shanghai · English · Original question
What ID should I bring for a mainland China SIM, and what call and data allowances should I check?

Apply for a SIM at a China Telecom, China Mobile or China Unicom outlet with a passport or Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card.

Applies when
General mainland China guidance; confirm the specific service with its current operator.
Check the latest official or operator guidance before use.
Confirm branch capability, handset compatibility and plan details with the carrier.
Does not cover
No eSIM availability or unrestricted-internet guarantee.
No instant activation, all-branch service or fixed-tariff guarantee.
Sources and context

## Publishing rules

- Keep the PNG dimensions unchanged; upload at their native size.
- For Stories and Reels, all title, panda-eye, URL and CTA content is held in the central 1080 × 1420 px safe area. Do not add platform overlays in that field.
- Preserve the supplied English copy exactly. Do not translate or crowd the cards with extra copy; captions carry optional trip details.
- Gold is a discovery accent only. It never communicates an essential status by itself.
- Use high-contrast cream or ink text; do not recolour the Guide, add a national flag, or imply official transport, visa, emergency or booking authority.

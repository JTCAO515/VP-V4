# VisePanda Guide pose library

These eight 1024 × 1024 PNG cutouts extend the owner-supplied VisePanda
character for product and social use. Each file has a transparent RGBA
background and may be placed on Cream, Butter, Plum, or Ink fields without
adding a second outline or drop shadow.

## Character constants

Every pose preserves the VisePanda Guide’s round white face and belly,
deep ink-brown ears, limbs, and eye patches, glossy open eyes, warm open
smile, dark-plum phone prop, cream sticker edge, and restrained plum and
antique-gold accents. The source visual reference is
`assets/brand/vise-panda/VP-正面全身.png`; it remains unchanged.

## Placement and handling

- Keep the full sticker contour visible and leave clear space of at least one
  eye diameter around the figure.
- Use one pose per message, card, or social composition; do not stack poses
  into a crowd or crop off the face, phone, or cream outline.
- Do not recolour, trace, add clothing, add words, or convert the poses into
  an authority badge. Keep the supplied file names stable for UI and social
  integrations.

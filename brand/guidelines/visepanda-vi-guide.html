VisePanda Visual identity guide · v1.0
Brand idea

Your warm
local friend
in China.

VisePanda helps international visitors make China feel exciting, navigable and full of small local magic. It speaks like a cheerful travel buddy—not a cold travel dashboard or an official authority.

01 · Logo rules

The panda is the guide.

Master lockup

Use the supplied SVG as the master. Keep the panda, phone, lower VisePanda ribbon and go2china.space ribbon together whenever space allows.

Clear space: leave at least one panda-eye diameter around the lockup. Minimum size: 120 px wide on screen or 32 mm in print.

Approved grounds: Cream, Butter, Plum-900 and Ink-950. Preserve the Cream sticker edge on dark fields.

DoUse the original mark at a generous size, with calm space and a single clear background.
Don’tStretch, squeeze, recolour, add glow, crowd, redraw or crop the master logo destructively.

03 · Typography
Display · Baloo 2 Semibold
China, made easy.
Body · Nunito Sans

Use short, welcoming language with simple sentence structure. For accurate Chinese place names, use Noto Sans as a compatible fallback. Avoid sharp tech type, condensed type and decorative scripts.

One panda. Eight helpful moods.

The Guide keeps its round white face, dark patches, wide delighted eyes, open smile, phone prop and Cream sticker edge. It is a helpful companion—not an official, a safety authority or a cultural costume.

05 · Illustration & icons
A little local magic

Draw the journey, not a stereotype.

Build illustrations from a scenic window, a simplified landmark appropriate to the actual destination, a hand-drawn cloud and a curved Gold route. Use quiet space. Landmark choices can include the Great Wall, Temple of Heaven, West Lake, Zhangjiajie, Shanghai skyline and Xi’an City Wall when contextually true.

07 · Social kit

Give every trip tip
a little personality.

Social layouts pair one short hook, a scenic window, one Panda Guide pose and a clear route/CTA. Keep important text, eyes and URLs within the platform safe area.

08 · Care in the details

Warm should still be clear.

  • Meet WCAG AA contrast for normal text.
  • Never use colour as the only state signal.
  • Label icon-only controls and provide image alt text.
  • Keep interactive targets at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Offer reduced motion; the Guide should never distract from travel information.

Copy rhythm

Be compact, encouraging and specific: “Your train leaves from Beijing South—give yourself 20 minutes for the station.” Avoid bureaucratic language, hype and unqualified guarantees.

09 · Handoff

Use the system with care.

1

Start with tokens

Use tokens/visepanda.tokens.json for colour, type, layout and material values.

2

Choose the right guide pose

Use the named IP state for the moment; never force a cheerful pose into an alert or accessibility message.

3

Ship with context

Pair every landmark, route and local tip with accurate destination context and accessible copy.

Brand idea

Your warm
local friend
in China.

VisePanda helps international visitors make China feel exciting, navigable and full of small local magic. It speaks like a cheerful travel buddy—not a cold travel dashboard or an official authority.

00 · Brand foundation

Guidance, not a dashboard.

The role

Archetype: The Guide, with the warmth of a Caregiver. VisePanda helps a visitor take the next useful step without sounding like an authority or a transaction funnel.

Promise: Make China feel easy, welcoming, and wonderfully yours.

One defining word: Reassurance.

Five human traits

Warm before clever. Resourceful when plans change. Playful without being distracting. Clear in every instruction. Respectful of travellers and local culture.

Unlike booking-led travel platforms, VisePanda stays beside the traveller as a local China guide before, during and after the itinerary.

01 · Logo rules

The panda is the guide.

Master lockup

Use the supplied SVG as the master. Keep the panda, phone, lower VisePanda ribbon and go2china.space ribbon together whenever space allows.

Clear space: leave at least one panda-eye diameter around the lockup. Minimum size: 120 px wide on screen or 32 mm in print.

Approved grounds: Cream, Butter, Plum-900 and Ink-950. Preserve the Cream sticker edge on dark fields.

DoUse the original mark at a generous size, with calm space and a single clear background.
Don’tStretch, squeeze, recolour, add glow, crowd, redraw or crop the master logo destructively.

Composition

Cream 45% · Plum 25% · Ink 15% · Gold 10% · Butter 5%.

Functional use

Cream for breathing room; Ink for text and edges; Plum for fields; Gold for routes and CTAs; Butter for welcome and highlights.

Never pair

Pure red + bright yellow, neon blue-purple gradients, or small Gold text on Cream. They weaken readability or pull the brand into the wrong category.

03 · Typography
Display · Baloo 2 Semibold
China, made easy.
Body · Nunito Sans

Use short, welcoming language with simple sentence structure. For accurate Chinese place names, use Noto Sans as a compatible fallback. Avoid sharp tech type, condensed type and decorative scripts.

Hero56–80 px / 0.95
Page title36–48 px / 1.05
Card title20–24 px / 1.2
Body16 px / 1.6
Support13–14 px / 1.45
CTA14–16 px / 700–800

Pairing: Baloo 2 only earns attention in headings; Nunito Sans handles everything readers must scan. Track display text slightly tight, body at normal spacing. Noto Sans is the multilingual fallback; Fredoka is the free display fallback.

03A · Voice & tagline

Kind, specific, and
ready with the next step.

Say this

“Start here—your first easy win in Beijing.”

“You’ve got this. The metro is two minutes away.”

“Your train leaves from Beijing South—give yourself 20 minutes for the station.”

Never say this

“Our AI solution optimizes your travel journey.”

“The ultimate China experience is guaranteed.”

Do not sound bureaucratic, overpromise, or imply official authority.

07 · Social kit

Give every trip tip
a little personality.

Social layouts pair one short hook, a scenic window, one Panda Guide pose and a clear route/CTA. Keep important text, eyes and URLs within the platform safe area.

Reusable formula

One 3–5 word English hook, one Guide pose, one Gold route, one accurate destination, and one CTA. Use 1:1 for destination starters, 4:5 for practical cheat sheets, 9:16 for Story/Reels and a Cream end card for video close.

Safe export

Keep titles, URLs, CTAs and panda eyes within the central safe area. Export native PNGs: topic-1080.png, topic-1080x1350.png, or topic-1080x1920.png.

08 · Care in the details

Warm should still be clear.

  • Meet WCAG AA contrast for normal text.
  • Never use colour as the only state signal.
  • Label icon-only controls and provide image alt text.
  • Keep interactive targets at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Offer reduced motion; the Guide should never distract from travel information.

Copy rhythm

Be compact, encouraging and specific: “Your train leaves from Beijing South—give yourself 20 minutes for the station.” Avoid bureaucratic language, hype and unqualified guarantees.

09 · Non-negotiables

Three rules that never move.

1

The panda is a friend.

Never imply official visa, transport, emergency, government or booking authority.

2

Cute serves clarity.

Routes, locations, CTAs and safety information always beat decoration.

3

Keep the signature.

Plum, Gold and the Cream sticker edge are core recognition assets—not optional styling.

10 · Handoff
